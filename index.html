ABOUT ME

I WORKED FOR NYPD. WAS A DETECTIVE. HAD A BADGE, A FAMILY, AND A FUTURE THAT DIDN'T LOOK LIKE A CRIME SCENE. THEN THE WORLD SHIFTED. VENGEANCE IS A LAZY MAN'S GAME, BUT I PLAYED IT UNTIL THE DECK WAS EMPTY. I WAS CHASING GHOSTS THROUGH A BLIZZARD THAT NEVER ENDED.

THE PAYBACK WAS REAL SWEET. I TRADED MY SOUL FOR A PILE OF COLD BODIES, AND WHEN THE SMOKE CLEARED, THE ONLY THING WAITING FOR ME WAS THE BOTTOM OF A BOTTLE. NOW? NOW I’M JUST AN AMERICAN 'HAS-BEEN', A WALKING SHADOW OF BETTER DAYS THAT I CAN'T EVEN REMEMBER CLEARLY ANYMORE.

MY JOB

My HOBBIES

MY HOBBIES COME IN ALL SHAPES AND SIZES. AND I’M NOT PICKY EITHER: A HANDFUL OF WHITE LIES AND A COLD WHISKEY CHASER TO WASH DOWN THE REGRET IS MORE THAN I CAN ASK FOR.

I ENJOY LONG WALKS THROUGH THE GUTTERS OF WHATEVER SHITHOLE I’M SEND INTO. I DON’T NEED A TOUR GUIDE; I JUST FOLLOW THE TRAIL OF CORRUPTION AND THE ECHOES OF DISTANT SIRENS. THE SCENERY NEVER CHANGES—IT’S ALWAYS DARK, ALWAYS RAINING, AND SOMEONE ALWAYS WANTS TO KILL THE TOURIST.
